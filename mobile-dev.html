Learning Roadmap

Nine phases from complete beginner to shipping real apps on the App Store and Google Play. Each phase has topics, tools, and real courses linked directly.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3
Programming Fundamentals
Before writing your first app, master the universal programming concepts that apply across every platform. Data structures, logic, and algorithms are the foundation every great mobile developer stands on.

Specializations

After the foundation, you pick your platform and go deep. Each track has its own ecosystem, hiring market, and salary ceiling.

iOS / Swift
🍎 iOS Development
Swift · SwiftUI · Apple Ecosystem
iOS developers build for the world's most valuable app ecosystem — Apple's App Store generates 63–65% of global app store revenue despite having fewer users than Android. iOS users spend significantly more on apps, making iOS the premium track. Senior iOS developers at Apple, Google, and top-tier startups earn $160K–$220K+. Cupertino alone averages $153K for iOS developers — the highest city salary for any mobile specialization.
Swift 5.9+ SwiftUI UIKit Xcode Core Data / SwiftData App Store Connect
Android / Kotlin
🤖 Android Development
Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Google
Android commands 72% of global smartphone market share — the largest installed base in human history. Android developers have the widest reach of any platform: emerging markets in Asia, Africa, and Latin America run almost entirely on Android. Google's Jetpack Compose has modernized Android development, and Kotlin Multiplatform is opening new career horizons. Senior Android developers at FAANG companies earn $170K–$250K in total compensation.
Kotlin Jetpack Compose Android Studio MVVM Architecture Kotlin Multiplatform Google Play Console
Flutter
🐦 Flutter Specialist
Dart · Cross-Platform · Google Backed
Flutter is the fastest-growing mobile framework — 42% market share in 2025, backed by Google, and used by over 1 million apps worldwide. Flutter developers command salaries averaging 7% higher than equivalent React Native developers. With a single codebase deploying to iOS, Android, Web, and Desktop simultaneously, Flutter dramatically reduces company costs — making Flutter developers extremely attractive to startups and enterprises alike.
Dart Flutter Widgets Riverpod / Bloc Firebase Animations API Platform Channels
React Native
⚛️ React Native Specialist
JavaScript · Meta Ecosystem · Most Jobs
React Native has the most job postings of any cross-platform framework — 6,413 US LinkedIn postings vs Flutter's 1,068. Used by Meta, Shopify, Microsoft, and Airbnb for flagship apps. For developers already familiar with JavaScript and React, React Native is the fastest path to shipping cross-platform mobile apps professionally. The new architecture (JSI, Fabric, TurboModules) brings performance on par with native apps.
JavaScript / TypeScript React Native Core Expo React Navigation Redux / Zustand New Architecture
Mobile Gaming
🎮 Mobile Gaming
Unity · Unreal · Games = 64% of App Revenue
Mobile games represent 64% of all app revenue — the single largest segment of the entire app economy. Unity powers over 70% of all mobile games worldwide. Game developers with Unity and mobile publishing experience are among the most creatively fulfilled engineers in the industry. Top mobile game studios at EA, Supercell, and King pay senior mobile game developers $140K–$200K, and successful indie developers earn millions through the App Store.
Unity 3D C# Scripting Game Physics In-App Purchases Ad Integration AR (ARKit / ARCore)

From Student to Expert

Career Growth Timeline

A realistic picture of your mobile development journey — from first line of Swift or Kotlin to shipping apps used by millions.

Learning Phase
Months 1–6 — Foundation, Platform & First Apps
Building Your Mobile Foundation
Study programming fundamentals, pick your primary platform (iOS or Android), and build 4–5 real apps. Ship at least one app to the App Store or Google Play — even a simple one. Document your apps publicly on GitHub. Earn your Meta or Google certification. Follow Apple WWDC sessions and Android dev blog. Start applying by month 5 — mobile portfolios get attention faster than most tech disciplines.
✓Platform chosen & core skills solid ✓First app live on App/Play Store ✓Meta or Google cert earned ✓GitHub portfolio published
First Opportunity
Months 6–10 — Internship / Junior Role
Your First Mobile Dev Job
💼 $40K–$70K (Internship) · $66K–$90K (Junior)
Apply for mobile development internships or junior iOS / Android / Flutter roles. A real shipped app on the stores dramatically improves interview rates. Freelance mobile projects on Upwork or Toptal ($50–$100/hr) are a valid entry path while job hunting. Begin learning your second platform or cross-platform framework — versatile mobile developers are significantly more hireable than single-platform specialists at entry level.
✓First mobile role or internship ✓Second platform learning started ✓First freelance client (optional) ✓Flutter or RN in progress
Early Career
Year 1 — Junior Mobile Engineer
iOS Engineer / Android Engineer / Flutter Developer
💰 $80K–$110K
You are shipping features in a real app used by real users — one of the most viscerally rewarding experiences in software engineering. You are fixing bugs, implementing design specs, and integrating backend APIs. Start owning whole features end-to-end. Deepen your specialization and pursue a cross-platform framework. At this stage, engineers who can ship independently are valued above those who always need guidance.
✓First full-time role secured ✓Features shipped to millions ✓Specialization deepening ✓Cross-platform skills added
Career Growth
Year 2–3 — Mid-Level Mobile Engineer
Mobile Engineer / iOS Engineer / Flutter Lead
💰 $110K–$150K
Mid-level mobile engineers own entire modules, architect solutions, and start mentoring juniors. You are making architectural decisions: which state management approach to use, how to structure navigation, how to optimize startup time. Engineers with dual-platform fluency (iOS + Android or Flutter) command a premium above single-platform peers. At this stage, side projects and apps you've independently shipped dramatically distinguish you.
✓Mid-level role secured ✓Owning modules end-to-end ✓Dual-platform or Flutter fluent ✓Mentoring junior devs
Senior Level
Year 3–5 — Senior Mobile Engineer
Senior iOS / Android / Flutter Engineer · Mobile Tech Lead
💰 $140K–$190K
Senior mobile engineers design the architecture of entire apps, lead platform migrations (e.g. UIKit → SwiftUI, or native → Flutter), and set the technical standards for a full team. At companies like Apple, Google, Meta, and Airbnb, senior mobile engineers earn $200K–$350K in total compensation. Freelance consulting at $100–$200/hr becomes very realistic at this level, opening substantial additional income streams.
✓Senior role secured ✓Leading architecture decisions ✓Consulting opportunities open ✓App with 100K+ active users
Expert / Leadership
Year 5+ — Staff / Principal / Indie Founder
Staff Engineer · Head of Mobile · Independent App Developer
💰 $180K–$280K+ · Unlimited (Indie)
The ceiling in mobile development is multidimensional. Staff and principal mobile engineers at top tech companies earn $250K–$400K+ in total compensation. But mobile also offers a unique path unavailable in most software disciplines: building your own apps and selling them independently. Successful indie app developers earn anywhere from side income to millions annually through the App Store — the creative ceiling is truly limitless. Every year, new indie developers hit the top charts.
✓Staff / Principal role or Indie ✓Industry recognition ✓$200K+ or own app revenue ✓Speaking / open source
