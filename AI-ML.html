Phase by Phase

Learning Roadmap

Nine structured phases from mathematics foundations to production AI systems. Each includes the topics, tools, and real courses that matter most.

🔢
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4
Math & Statistics Foundations
Machine learning is built on mathematics. You don't need a PhD, but a solid grasp of linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics is non-negotiable for understanding why models work — and why they fail.
Linear Algebra Matrices & Vectors Calculus & Derivatives Gradient Descent Probability Theory Statistics & Distributions Bayes' Theorem Information Theory
📚 Courses
  • Mathematics for Machine Learning — Imperial College / Coursera
  • Linear Algebra — Khan Academy (Free)
🐍
Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8
Python for AI/ML
Python is the undisputed language of AI. Learn the language itself and the core scientific libraries that power every ML pipeline in the world — from data loading to model training.
Python Fundamentals NumPy & Arrays Pandas DataFrames Matplotlib & Seaborn Jupyter Notebooks Scikit-learn basics Data Cleaning & EDA
📚 Courses
  • Python for Data Science, AI & Development — IBM/Coursera (Free)
  • Python & Pandas — Kaggle Learn (Free)
🧠
Phase 3 · Weeks 9–13
Machine Learning Fundamentals
Supervised learning, unsupervised learning, model evaluation — this is the core of ML. Master the classic algorithms that still power real production systems alongside modern deep learning techniques.
Supervised Learning Linear & Logistic Regression Decision Trees / Random Forest SVM & KNN Unsupervised Learning Clustering & PCA Model Evaluation & Cross-Val Feature Engineering
📚 Courses
  • Machine Learning Specialization — Andrew Ng / Stanford / Coursera
  • Intro to Machine Learning — Kaggle Learn (Free)
🔗
Phase 4 · Months 4–5
Deep Learning & Neural Networks
Deep learning is what separates AI engineers from data analysts. Learn how neural networks think, how to build them from scratch, and how to train them at scale using the dominant frameworks.
Neural Network Architecture Backpropagation Activation Functions CNNs (Convolutional) RNNs & LSTMs PyTorch Fundamentals TensorFlow / Keras Transfer Learning
📚 Courses
  • Deep Learning Specialization — Andrew Ng / DeepLearning.AI / Coursera
  • Practical Deep Learning for Coders — fast.ai (Free)
🗣️
Phase 5 · Month 6
NLP & Large Language Models
NLP is the most requested AI skill in job postings globally. Transformers, BERT, and GPT-style models now underpin virtually every AI product in production — this phase is where you learn to work with them directly.
Transformer Architecture Attention Mechanism BERT & GPT Models Hugging Face Transformers Fine-tuning (LoRA / QLoRA) Text Classification & NER Sentiment Analysis Tokenization
📚 Courses
  • Natural Language Processing Specialization — DeepLearning.AI / Coursera
  • HuggingFace NLP Course — Hugging Face (Free)
✨
Phase 6 · Month 7
Generative AI & LLM Engineering
GenAI expertise adds a 40–60% salary premium over baseline ML skills. This phase covers the bleeding edge — RAG systems, LLM agents, prompt engineering, and building real AI-powered applications with foundation models.
Prompt Engineering RAG Architecture Vector Databases LangChain / LlamaIndex LLM Agents OpenAI / Anthropic APIs LLM Evaluation RLHF Basics
📚 Courses
  • Short Courses — GenAI & LLMs — DeepLearning.AI (Free)
  • Generative AI Engineering with LLMs — IBM/Coursera
👁️
Phase 7 · Month 8
Computer Vision
Computer vision powers self-driving cars, medical imaging, facial recognition, and robotics. Junior Computer Vision engineers already command some of the highest entry-level salaries in all of AI at $121K+.
Image Classification Object Detection (YOLO) Semantic Segmentation OpenCV GANs & Diffusion Models Vision Transformers (ViT) Data Augmentation
📚 Courses
  • OpenCV Free Course — OpenCV University (Free)
  • Convolutional Neural Networks — DeepLearning.AI / Coursera
🚀
Phase 8 · Month 9
MLOps & Model Deployment
A model that never ships to production creates zero value. MLOps skills add a 25–40% salary premium and are the biggest bottleneck in the industry — companies struggle to find engineers who can take models from experiment to production reliably.
ML Pipelines Docker & Kubernetes AWS SageMaker / Vertex AI MLflow & Experiment Tracking Model Monitoring CI/CD for ML FastAPI Model Serving
📚 Courses
  • ML Engineering for Production (MLOps) — DeepLearning.AI / Coursera
  • ML DevOps Engineer Nanodegree — Udacity
🎯
Phase 9 · Month 10+

You now have a complete foundation. Go deep into one domain — each is a distinct career with its own tools, research frontier, and salary ceiling. Pick the one that matches your curiosity.

Specializations

AI/ML branches into highly distinct disciplines. Each has its own research frontier, toolset, and salary ceiling — over 75% of job listings specifically seek domain experts.

NLP / LLMs
🗣️ NLP & LLM Engineering
Language AI · The Highest-Demand Specialization
NLP appears in 19.7% of all AI job postings — the single most requested AI specialization. NLP engineers build the systems behind chatbots, document analysis, search, and language understanding. With the explosion of LLMs like GPT and Claude, engineers who can fine-tune, evaluate, and deploy language models are commanding $170K+ at mid-level. GenAI expertise alone adds a 40–60% salary premium above baseline ML engineers.
Transformers (BERT/GPT) Hugging Face LangChain RAG Systems Vector DBs Fine-tuning / LoRA
Computer Vision
👁️ Computer Vision
Visual AI · Self-Driving Cars · Medical Imaging
Computer vision engineers build systems that help machines see and understand the world. From facial recognition and autonomous vehicles to medical imaging diagnostics and quality control in manufacturing — CV powers it all. Junior CV engineers already command the highest entry-level salaries in AI at $121K+, with senior specialists reaching $211K. The rise of diffusion models and vision transformers has made this specialization even hotter in 2025.
CNNs & ViTs YOLO / Object Detection OpenCV Image Segmentation GANs / Diffusion TensorFlow / PyTorch
Reinforcement Learning
🤖 Reinforcement Learning
RL · Agents That Learn by Doing
Reinforcement learning is what powers AlphaGo, game-playing AI, robotics control systems, and increasingly — the RLHF training that shapes modern LLMs like ChatGPT. It is the most mathematically demanding specialization in ML and the one with the fewest qualified engineers — making specialists extremely valuable. Companies like DeepMind, OpenAI, and NVIDIA are leading employers in this space.
Markov Decision Processes Q-Learning / DQN Policy Gradient PPO / SAC OpenAI Gym RLHF
MLOps
🚀 MLOps & AI Engineering
Production AI · The Biggest Skills Gap
MLOps is the single biggest bottleneck in the industry. Companies have models — they can't reliably deploy or maintain them. MLOps engineers build the pipelines, infrastructure, and monitoring systems that take AI from a Jupyter notebook to a production system serving millions of users. MLOps skills add a 25–40% salary premium and are increasingly required at every level of ML engineering.
AWS SageMaker Vertex AI MLflow Docker / Kubernetes Airflow / Kubeflow Model Monitoring
Generative AI
✨ Generative AI
GenAI · The Fastest-Growing Segment
Generative AI is the single fastest-growing segment of the entire tech industry in 2025. Engineers who specialize in building GenAI applications — whether image generation, video synthesis, or AI-powered creative tools — are working at the bleeding edge. Professionals specializing in GenAI average $174K annually, with top performers at leading AI labs exceeding $300K. Demand for prompt engineers alone grew 135% in one year.
Stable Diffusion DALL-E / Midjourney API LLM Fine-tuning Multimodal Models ComfyUI Prompt Engineering

Career Growth Timeline

A realistic picture of your AI/ML journey. This field rewards depth over breadth — the longer you stay and specialize, the more your value compounds exponentially.

Learning Phase
Months 1–8 — Foundations, ML, and Deep Learning
Building Your AI Foundation
Study math, Python, and classic ML algorithms. Complete Andrew Ng's Machine Learning Specialization and the Deep Learning Specialization. Build at least 3 end-to-end projects on Kaggle or GitHub. Begin competing in Kaggle competitions — even bronze medals look excellent on a resume. Document your learning publicly on LinkedIn.
✓ML Specialization completed ✓3 Kaggle projects shipped ✓GitHub portfolio active ✓First Kaggle competition
First Opportunity
Months 9–14 — Internship / Junior Role
Your First AI/ML Job
💼 $60K–$100K (Internship / Junior Researcher)
Apply for ML engineering internships, junior data scientist roles, or research assistant positions. Unlike web development, AI/ML internships are often research-heavy and may require academic connections. Build your reputation on Kaggle, contribute to Hugging Face models, and publish a write-up or GitHub project that demonstrates original thinking.
✓First ML internship landed ✓Kaggle Master / Expert rank ✓Published ML write-up ✓Intermediate cert earned
Early Career
Year 1–2 — Junior ML Engineer
ML Engineer / AI Engineer / Data Scientist
💰 $100K–$150K
Junior ML engineers already start significantly above most tech roles. You are building, training, and evaluating models for real business problems — recommendation systems, fraud detection, churn prediction, or NLP pipelines. Start specializing in either NLP, Computer Vision, or MLOps based on your interests and company needs. Cloud certification at this stage opens more senior doors.
✓First ML role secured ✓Production model deployed ✓Specialization chosen ✓AWS / Azure cert in progress

Year 3–5 — Mid-Level ML Engineer
Senior ML Engineer / NLP Engineer / CV Engineer / MLOps Engineer
💰 $149K–$200K
Mid-level ML engineers with 3–5 years of experience and specialization are among the most sought-after professionals in all of tech. The jump from junior to mid-level is approximately 29% salary increase. You are now designing full ML systems, leading model development, and owning production models. LLM or GenAI expertise at this stage pushes compensation significantly above baseline.
✓Mid-level role secured ✓Google or AWS cloud cert ✓System design ownership ✓Mentoring junior engineers

Senior ML Engineer / AI Research Engineer / Staff Engineer
💰 $175K–$275K
Senior ML engineers design complete AI systems end-to-end and own critical production models that impact millions of users. At this level you are defining technical vision, establishing team standards, and making architectural decisions that span years. Companies like Apple, Google, Meta, and NVIDIA are paying their senior ML engineers $300K–$400K+ in total compensation at this level.

Expert / Leadership

Principal Engineer / AI Research Scientist / Head of AI / CTO Track

The ceiling in AI/ML is among the highest in all of human employment. Demand to supply
AI/ML engineering is the highest-ceiling, highest-demand career in the history of computer science. The median salary for an AI/ML engineer in the US is $187,500 — and junior engineers already start at $100K+. With 78% of positions targeting professionals with real specialization and a 3:1 demand-to-supply ratio, every engineer who commits to this path and goes deep in a specialization enters one of the most seller's markets in all of tech. The ML market is projected to reach $503 billion by 2030, the World Economic Forum projects 170 million new AI-related jobs globally by that year, and 86% of employers expect AI to fundamentally transform their business. The engineers who understand how to build, deploy, and maintain these systems will be the most valuable people in the digital economy.
