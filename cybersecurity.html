Learning Roadmap

Nine phases from complete beginner to specialization-ready. Each phase has topics, subtopics, and real courses linked directly.

🖥️
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3
OS Fundamentals
Every attack and defense happens on an OS. Learn Linux deeply — the backbone of security tools. Also understand Windows internals that dominate enterprise networks.
Linux File SystemCLI & Bash File PermissionsProcesses & Daemons User ManagementWindows Registry Active DirectorySSH & Remote Access
📚 Courses
  • Linux Fundamentals for IT Professionals — Coursera (Free)
  • Pre-Security Path — TryHackMe (Free)
🌐
Phase 2 · Weeks 4–6
Networking Fundamentals
The internet is packets. Understanding how data travels and can be intercepted is non-negotiable — whether attacking or defending.
OSI & TCP/IPIP & Subnetting DNS, DHCP, HTTP/SFirewalls & ACLs WiresharkVPN & Proxy Routing & SwitchingNAT & Port Fwd
📚 Courses
  • Bits & Bytes of Computer Networking — Google/Coursera
  • CompTIA Network+ Full Course — Prof. Messer (Free)
🔐
Phase 3 · Weeks 7–9
Security Fundamentals
Learn the core language of security — CIA Triad, threat models, and the math behind encryption protecting all digital communication.
CIA TriadAAA Model Symmetric EncryptionHashing (MD5/SHA) PKI & CertificatesSSL/TLS Malware TypesSocial Engineering

  • Google Cybersecurity Certificate — Coursera

Specializations

After the foundation, you branch into a focused domain. Each has its own tools, job market, and career ceiling.

Red Team
⚔️ Offensive Security
Red Team · Think Like The Attacker
Penetration testers are paid to break into systems legally. One of the most in-demand and highest-paid tracks. OSCP holders average $130K+ and elite red teamers exceed $200K. The offensive mindset is the most sought-after skill in all of cybersecurity.
MetasploitBurp Suite NmapCobalt Strike Privilege EscalationReport Writing
Blue Team
🛡️ Defensive Security
Blue Team · Build The Fortress
The backbone of every security team. SOC Analyst is the most common entry-level role in cybersecurity — the most accessible starting point with clear growth into Threat Hunter and IR Lead roles at $90K–$140K.
Splunk / SIEMWireshark SnortThreat Hunting IR PlaybooksMITRE ATT&CK
Purple Team
💜 Purple Teaming
Red + Blue · Bridge Both Worlds
The hottest specialization of 2025. Purple teamers use attacker techniques to improve detection logic. Hybrid-skilled professionals earn an 18% salary premium over single-track specialists and are increasingly sought by mature security programs.
ATT&CK NavigatorAtomic Red Team CALDERADetection Engineering Adversary Emulation
Cloud Security
☁️ Cloud Security
Cloud · Secure The New Infrastructure
As organizations migrate to AWS, Azure, and GCP, cloud security engineers are among the most urgently needed professionals globally. This role pays $140K–$180K at major companies and is growing faster than any other security specialization.
AWS IAMAzure Defender TerraformZero Trust DevSecOpsContainer Security
Web / AppSec
🌐 Web / App Security
AppSec · Secure From The Code Up
Every web application has vulnerabilities. This track also has the most accessible entry point through bug bounty platforms — where you can earn real money before even landing a job. Top bug bounty hunters earn $300K+ annually.
Burp Suite ProOWASP Top 10 OWASP ZAPAPI Testing ffufBug Bounty

Career Growth Timeline

A realistic picture of your journey — from first study session to senior specialist and beyond.

Learning Phase
Months 1–6 — Foundation & Core Skills
Building Your Base
Study OS, networking, scripting, and security fundamentals. Complete TryHackMe rooms daily. Pass CompTIA Network+ and Security+. Set up a home lab. Document your journey on LinkedIn and GitHub. No income yet — invest time now for leverage later.
✓Network+ & Security+ earned ✓Home lab running ✓TryHackMe profile active ✓LinkedIn profile built
First Opportunity
Months 6–9 — Internship / Junior Role
Your First Security Job
💼 $30K–$60K (Internship) · $55K–$80K (Junior)
Apply for remote internships or SOC Analyst Tier 1 positions. Security+ plus hands-on lab experience is enough to get interviews. Blue team roles are the most accessible entry point. Pursue eJPT or CySA+ to strengthen your application simultaneously.
✓First role or internship landed ✓eJPT or CySA+ in progress ✓Real incident experience ✓GitHub portfolio published
Early Career
Year 1 — Entry-Level Professional
SOC Analyst / Junior Pentester
💰 $65K–$95K
You're now employed in security. Daily responsibilities include monitoring SIEM dashboards, triaging alerts, writing reports, and collaborating with senior engineers. Start specializing and pursue your chosen track. CEH or AWS Security Specialty are excellent targets here.
✓Specialization chosen ✓Intermediate cert earned ✓First pen test report written
Career Growth
Year 2 — Mid-Level Specialist
Security Engineer / Penetration Tester / Threat Hunter
💰 $90K–$130K
With a year of real experience, you move into mid-level roles with significantly higher autonomy and pay. You're leading engagements, mentoring juniors, and driving technical decisions. OSCP holders at this stage are positioned for top consulting firms and high-value clients.
✓Mid-level role secured ✓OSCP or eCPPT earned ✓Leading engagements ✓Mentoring juniors
Senior Level
Year 3 — Senior Specialist
Senior Security Engineer / Red Team Lead / Cloud Security Architect
💰 $120K–$175K
Three years in, you design security architectures, lead red team operations, or build cloud security programs. CISSP or CISM holders are fast-tracked toward management. Freelance consulting at $100–$200/hr becomes a realistic side income at this level.
✓Senior role secured ✓CISSP or CISM in progress ✓Consulting opportunities
Expert / Leadership
Year 4–5 — Expert / Leadership Track
Principal Engineer / CISO Track / Independent Consultant
💰 $160K–$250K+
The ceiling in cybersecurity is among the highest in all of tech. Principal engineers, CISO officers, and independent consultants routinely exceed $200K. Elite bug bounty hunters earn $300K+ annually. Every industry is digitalizing — demand will only grow.
✓Leadership or C-suite path ✓Industry recognition ✓$200K+ achievable
